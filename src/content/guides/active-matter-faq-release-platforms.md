## When does Active Matter launch?

The developer’s official announcements say that Active Matter launches on September 15, 2026 on Steam, Xbox Series X|S, and PlayStation 5. Platform storefront timing can vary, so check the platform page on launch day rather than relying on a converted countdown.

## What kind of game is Active Matter?

The official Steam description presents it as a realistic military shooter set in a fractured multiverse, with raids for loot and PvP battles over active matter. In practical terms, players should expect a loop that combines preparation, objectives, combat, resource decisions, and extraction.

## Is Active Matter available on PC?

Yes. The official launch announcement includes Steam, and the Steam store page is the primary PC listing. If you played an earlier version through the Gaijin Store, the developers have published instructions about access to the Steam version and account linking; follow the current official instructions for your account rather than using an old key or login guide.

## Is Active Matter on PlayStation and Xbox?

The official announcement lists PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S alongside Steam for the September 15 launch. Older console posts, previews, or demo pages may describe a different access phase, so confirm availability in the current store listing for your region.

## Is cross-progression supported?

The official Active Matter community announcement says players who previously purchased the Early Version through the Gaijin Store can receive access on Steam and link progress between the Gaijin Store and Steam versions. Account linking is a sensitive operation: read the official prompt carefully and do not overwrite a save unless you understand which progress will be kept.

## Will there be a wipe?

Do not treat a community comment as a permanent wipe policy. The official site and announcements are the sources to check for a launch wipe, seasonal reset, or progression change. Until a current developer notice says otherwise, spend launch-week resources as if the economy may be tuned, and avoid building a strategy around one unconfirmed promise.

## What changed before launch?

The official news feed has documented several pre-launch fixes and improvements, including Shelter customization work and fixes for issues such as reloading animation desync, hit registration, black-screen hangs, and enemies interacting through walls. These notes show that the game is actively changing; expect some guides to need updates after launch.

## What should a new player do first?

Start with a low-cost kit, learn one objective, identify one extraction route, and leave before the bag or squad becomes difficult to manage. Use the first three raids to learn navigation, economy, and pressure separately. That gives you a baseline before you chase a weapon ranking or a high-risk PvP route.

## Which source should I trust?

Use the [official Active Matter site](https://activematter.game/en/) and [official news feed](https://activematter.game/en/news) for release timing, platform announcements, and patch changes. Use the [official Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2887580/Active_Matter/) for the store description and PC listing. Community guides can be useful for observations, but label them as community findings and check their build number.
